ABOUT ME

My discovery of data science occured in college at Rutgers University where I obtained a bachelors in Computer Science with a double minor in Statistics and Data Science. The course work as well as my research in topic-analysis on long covid symptoms over time under Dr. Amari led me to pursue a masters in the field.

As of March 2025, I am on track to complete my masters in Data Science and Analytics at Georgetown University this spring and excited for what is to come next!

PROJECTS

A timeline of projects throughout my masters program

🦠 Long Covid Analysis 🦠

A data analysis on long covid and what demographics are most affected as well as its current perception in media and news coverage

Data Science and Analytics | Fall 2023

🎶 KPOP on the Western Music Industry 🎶

A time series study on KPOP in the western music market, answering questions on its current popularity as well as longevity

Time Series | Fall 2023

🎥 Unlocking Movie Magic: Predicting Film Success 🎥

Using data analytics and statistical modeling, we uncovered what aspects of a movie contribute to its overall success

Statistical Analysis | Spring 2024

🚗 Moving and Parking Violations in D.C. 🚗

An interactive data visualization newsletter for D.C. drivers on moving and parking data

Data Visualization | Spring 2024

🍎 Diet Culture Reddit Analysis 🍎

Using big data in the form of subreddit information and cloud computing, we analyzed how diet culture is spread online and the reasons why people seek such communities

Big Data and Cloud Computing | Fall 2024

🌙 Autonomous Lunar Rover Simulation 🌙

Using reinforcement learning techniques to simulate an autonomous lunar rover as it learns to gather resources in its environment

Reinforcement Learning | Fall 2024

🗞️ Information Retrieval: CrisisFACTS 🗞️

Machine Learning App Deployement | Spring 2024
